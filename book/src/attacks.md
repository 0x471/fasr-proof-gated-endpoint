# Attack catalogue

> **In short**
> - Each scenario records its result and evidence, from our tests or reviews.
> - Read the version and evidence level before interpreting a result.
> - Remaining attacks concern signalling or access to the device; the stock-system exposures are replaced in v1.

"Stopped" means the endpoint refused or neutralised the attack, supported by a test or Pi run. It does not mean the device stopped working. "Allowed on purpose" means accepted by design; "Still works" means information or an attack gets through, with a rate where measured.

The Version column identifies the system tested. Level distinguishes Pi measurements, automated tests and proposals. Result describes the outcome; the final column identifies its test. The full catalogue below keeps every scenario and qualification.
